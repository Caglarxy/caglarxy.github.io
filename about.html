About LPF.one

Thank you for using my software!

Les Petites Fugues is a personal project creating simple, useful software for the Mac. LPF Organizer and LPF Explorer are designed to make working with photo collections a little easier.

I welcome feedback, suggestions, and bug reports. If something isn't working as expected or you have an idea for improvement, please feel free to contact me.

You can reach me at info@lpf.one .

LPF.one software is a private project and is provided free of charge. I strive to answer inquiries and fix bugs whenever possible. However, please understand that there is no guarantee of support, response times, bug fixes, or future updates.

Thank you for your interest. I hope you enjoy using the software.
